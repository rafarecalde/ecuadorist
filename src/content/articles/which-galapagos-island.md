Pick the town you want to wake up in. The wildlife is not evenly assigned, and neither are the flights.

<div class="compare cols-3">
<article>
<h3>Santa Cruz</h3>
<p>Puerto Ayora is the busiest town in the islands. The Charles Darwin Research Station is here. The airport is not: you land at Baltra and cross to the island. Day boats leave from this harbor more often than from the others. If you want the most chances to be on the water without changing hotels, this is the base. The <a href="/book/galapagos-day-tours-santa-cruz/">Santa Cruz day tours</a> are the booked version.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>San Cristóbal</h3>
<p>Puerto Baquerizo Moreno is the provincial capital. The airport is on the island, which makes the first and last day simpler. It is a real town, sea lions on the waterfront included, and it is quieter than Puerto Ayora. A week that starts and ends on the same island, with no dawn crossing, fits here.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>Isabela</h3>
<p>The largest island, and the one people describe as slower. Puerto Villamil is the town. Most land travelers reach it by boat from Santa Cruz, not by the mainland jet. Use it as a second stop, not as the island you must leave on the morning of a flight home. The <a href="/visit/galapagos-without-a-cruise/">hopping guide</a> is the boat warning from the FCDO.</p>
</article>
</div>

## A short way to choose

<ol class="route">
<li><strong>First time, one island</strong><span>Santa Cruz, if day boats are the point. San Cristóbal, if you want the airport in town.</span></li>
<li><strong>A week and a second bed</strong><span>Santa Cruz plus Isabela, or San Cristóbal plus Santa Cruz. Three islands in a week is luggage.</span></li>
<li><strong>A cruise instead</strong><span>Then the town matters less. The ship is the base, and these three are ports, not the whole map. The <a href="/visit/galapagos/">islands chapter</a> is that choice.</span></li>
</ol>

The $200 park fee and the $20 transit card do not change with the island. They are in the [cost chapter](/visit/galapagos-trip-cost/). The [two-week route](/visit/two-weeks-ecuador-galapagos/) is where a mainland week sits in front of this decision.
