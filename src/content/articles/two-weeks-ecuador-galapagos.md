Two weeks is a mainland first act and an island second act. A cruise replaces the second act. It does not fit beside it.

<ol class="route">
<li><strong>Days 1–3 · Quito</strong><span>The <a href="/visit/quito-airport-arrival/">landing</a>, a quiet first day for the <a href="/visit/quito-altitude-sickness/">altitude</a>, then the old town or <a href="/book/quito-city-and-mitad-del-mundo-tours/">Mitad del Mundo</a>.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 4 · One highland day</strong><span><a href="/visit/cotopaxi/">Cotopaxi</a> or <a href="/visit/otavalo/">Otavalo</a>, not both. The booking pages are <a href="/book/cotopaxi-tours-from-quito/">Cotopaxi</a> and <a href="/book/otavalo-tours-from-quito/">Otavalo</a>.</span></li>
<li><strong>Days 5–6 · Cuenca, or a second Quito night</strong><span>If the flights to the islands leave from Quito, do not strand yourself in the south. If they leave cleanly from Guayaquil, read the <a href="/visit/is-ecuador-safe/">safety chapter</a> before you build a night there. Airside transit is the UK exception. A city night is not.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 7 · Fly to the islands</strong><span>Quito or Guayaquil to Baltra, for Santa Cruz, or to San Cristóbal. Same-day connections are the ones that work. The <a href="/visit/ecuador-entry-requirements/">entry chapter</a> and the <a href="/visit/galapagos-trip-cost/">fee chapter</a> are the paperwork: transit card before the island flight, park fee on arrival.</span></li>
<li><strong>Days 8–12 · Two islands, not four</strong><span>Sleep on <a href="/visit/which-galapagos-island/">Santa Cruz or San Cristóbal</a>, and add Isabela only if the boat is a reputable one. The <a href="/visit/galapagos-without-a-cruise/">island-hopping guide</a> is the method. Day tours from Santa Cruz are the <a href="/book/galapagos-day-tours-santa-cruz/">booking page</a>.</span></li>
<li><strong>Days 13–14 · Back to the mainland, then home</strong><span>A night in Quito if the international flight is the next morning. Do not book the island flight and the flight home on the same afternoon.</span></li>
</ol>

The [Galápagos chapter](/visit/galapagos/) is the wildlife and the rules. The [cost chapter](/visit/galapagos-trip-cost/) is why this week costs more than the highland week, without a made-up total. The [best time](/visit/best-time-to-visit-ecuador/) is how the garúa months differ from the warm ones.
