Ecuador can show you a penguin and a glacier in the same week. The animals are not performing. The way you stand is part of whether they remain.

## The islands

On the [Galápagos](/visit/galapagos/), blue-footed boobies, marine iguanas, giant tortoises, and sea lions are the everyday miracles. Frigatebirds hang over the docks. The Galápagos penguin, the only penguin that lives on the equator, is a colder-water sight, often around Isabela and Fernandina.

The park’s distance rules are the whole ethic. Two meters is a common statement of it; follow whatever your naturalist says on that site, that day. Do not touch, feed, or surround an animal for a picture. If it walks toward you, you are the one who moves.

## Cloud forest and páramo

[Mindo](/visit/mindo/) is the birding capital of the western slope. Hummingbirds are the guaranteed audience. Toucans and the cock-of-the-rock are the reason people get up in the dark. Go with a local guide for the lek.

The Andean condor still flies over parts of the high páramo. It is rare. A day at Cotopaxi or Chimborazo is not a condor appointment. It is a chance.

## The forest

In the [Amazon](/visit/the-amazon/), expect monkeys, macaws, hoatzins, caimans, and, with luck, a pink river dolphin in Cuyabeno. Yasuní’s reputation rests on sheer numbers of species, many of them insects and birds you will not identify. That is a reason to hire a naturalist, not a reason to leave the trail.

Do not feed anything. Skip the flash. The best sighting of the trip is usually the one you did not rearrange.
