The money in Quito eats in the valley as often as it eats in town. Cumbayá is a short ride east, lower and warmer than the plaza. These three are the tables. The [Quito chapter](/visit/quito/) is the city above, and the dining rooms in town are in [where to eat in Quito](/eat/quito-restaurants/).
