Ten days is Quito, one volcano, one market or one forest, and Cuenca. It is not the Amazon and the islands and the beach. Those are other trips.

<ol class="route">
<li><strong>Days 1–2 · Quito</strong><span>Land, sleep, walk. The <a href="/visit/quito-altitude-sickness/">altitude chapter</a> is the reason day one is short. The <a href="/visit/quito-airport-arrival/">airport chapter</a> is the ride in. Old town on day two: plazas, not the cable car.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 3 · The city, or the middle of the world</strong><span>A guided Quito day, or Mitad del Mundo, once your head is quiet. The <a href="/book/quito-city-and-mitad-del-mundo-tours/">booking page</a> is the list. The <a href="/eat/quito-neighborhoods/">neighborhoods</a> tell you where to sleep.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 4 · Cotopaxi</strong><span>A day from Quito to the park and Laguna Limpiopungo, not a summit. The refuge is near 4,800 meters. The <a href="/book/cotopaxi-tours-from-quito/">Cotopaxi tours</a> are the booked version. The <a href="/visit/cotopaxi/">place chapter</a> is the mountain.</span></li>
<li><strong>Days 5–6 · Otavalo or Mindo</strong><span>North for the Saturday market, or west for a night in cloud forest. Do not do both. <a href="/book/otavalo-tours-from-quito/">Otavalo</a> and <a href="/book/mindo-tours-from-quito/">Mindo</a> are the day pages. <a href="/visit/otavalo/">Otavalo</a> and <a href="/visit/mindo/">Mindo</a> are the places.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 7 · Quilotoa, if the lungs agree</strong><span>The rim is near 3,900 meters. Skip it if Cotopaxi already hurt. Otherwise it is the crater on the way south. <a href="/book/quilotoa-tours-from-quito/">Quilotoa tours</a> if you are still based in Quito. The <a href="/visit/quilotoa/">lake chapter</a> if you want the walk down.</span></li>
<li><strong>Days 8–9 · Cuenca</strong><span>The southern city, lower than Quito, walkable center. <a href="/visit/cuenca/">The Cuenca chapter</a> is the pace. El Cajas is a high park beside it, not a rest day.</span></li>
<li><strong>Day 10 · Out</strong><span>Fly from Cuenca or back through Quito. A rental car for the highland road is the <a href="/book/quito-airport-car-rental/">airport rental page</a>, and only if someone in the car is happy at this altitude.</span></li>
</ol>

June to September is the sierra’s long dry season, in the [month-by-month chapter](/visit/best-time-to-visit-ecuador/). The [safety map](/visit/is-ecuador-safe/) is why this route never drops to the coast. If the trip grows by four days, the [two-week version](/visit/two-weeks-ecuador-galapagos/) is the one that earns the islands.

<p class="sources">Sources: the heights and the place descriptions are the ones in the city and park chapters of this guide. The decision to leave the coast off the route follows the area warnings in the <a href="/visit/is-ecuador-safe/">safety chapter</a>. Driving times are not printed.</p>
