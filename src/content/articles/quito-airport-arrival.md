Mariscal Sucre (UIO) is in Tababela, on the road toward Yaruquí. You are not a short hop from the old town. The first hour is also the [altitude hour](/visit/quito-altitude-sickness/).

<div class="compare cols-3">
<article>
<h3>Taxi</h3>
<p>The airport’s own page names two services at the terminal entrance and exit: Cooperativa Aeropuerto Mariscal Sucre No. 34 and Asociación de Taxistas del Aeropuerto Mariscal Sucre. It says they run 24 hours, every day. The UK FCDO says authorized taxi booths exist at Quito’s international airport, and that a yellow taxi in Quito should reset the meter. Agree the trip before you sit down if a meter is not the method. This page does not print a fare. The booth does.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>Uber</h3>
<p>Uber’s page for UIO says you cannot request a taxi through Uber there. It says you can request other options, including UberX, with the price shown up front and pickup directions in the app. Match the car and the plate before you get in. A price on a blog is already wrong. The price in the app, for that hour, is the one that exists.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>Private transfer</h3>
<p><a href="https://uiotransfers.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UIO Transfers</a> is a private car from the airport, booked before you land. It belongs to the owner of this site. It is not a sponsored placement, and it is not a taxi rank. The price, the meeting point, and what happens if the flight is late are on that site. Use it when you want the decision finished before you leave the jetway.</p>
</article>
</div>

## The drive

The airport says the ride to north Quito, south Quito, or downtown takes 35 to 50 minutes in moderate traffic. Night, rain, and a match day change that. The road is the eastern approach, then the city. Tell the driver the neighborhood, not just “Quito.” The [neighborhood chapter](/eat/quito-neighborhoods/) is the list of names that actually mean something.

If you are renting a car instead of riding, that is a different counter. The [airport rental page](/book/quito-airport-car-rental/) is the booking version. Driving yourself on the evening you land, at this altitude, is a choice. It is not the default in the [ten-day plan](/visit/ten-days-in-ecuador/).

## What to have in your hand

Passport stamp or the copy you were told to carry, the address in Spanish, and a way to get online if the app is the plan. An eSIM installed before the flight is the calm version. The [eSIM page](/book/ecuador-esim/) compares that. The [entry rules](/visit/ecuador-entry-requirements/) are the documents. The [safety chapter](/visit/is-ecuador-safe/) is why the official taxi booth exists as a sentence in a government travel page.
