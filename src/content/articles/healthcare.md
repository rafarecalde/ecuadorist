This is not medical advice. It is a description of how care is organized, so a visitor or a new resident knows where to start.

## Private hospitals

Foreign residents in [Quito](/visit/quito/) and [Cuenca](/visit/cuenca/) generally use private clinics. Hospital Metropolitano in Quito is a long-established private hospital. Cuenca has several private hospitals of its own. Quality in those two cities is the reason people name them in the [cities chapter](/retire/best-cities/). Outside them, emergency care exists and specialist care means a drive.

Many doctors in private practice in the two cities speak some English. Do not count on it at a public pharmacy or a small-town clinic. Bring the generic names of your medicines.

## Pharmacies and insurance

Pharmacies are widespread. Fybeca is one of the national chains. Some drugs that require a prescription at home are easier to buy here, and some specialty drugs are simply not on the shelf. Carry a supply of anything you cannot interrupt, and a list.

Visitors should carry travel medical insurance that covers evacuation. Residents often use a local private plan, an international plan, or both. A residence visa typically asks for health insurance that is valid in Ecuador. That requirement is explained on the official visa pages linked from the [visa chapter](/retire/visas/). Read the policy, not the slogan.

## IESS

IESS is the public social-security system. Some foreign residents affiliate and use its hospitals and clinics. It is a real system, with waits and with coverage that can be broad. It is not a concierge practice. Rules for voluntary affiliation change. Confirm them with IESS if you plan to join.

## Altitude and the Amazon

Quito’s altitude is a health fact, not a lifestyle detail. People with heart or lung conditions should talk to their own doctor before a move, not after a listing. For the [Amazon](/visit/the-amazon/), see a travel clinic about yellow fever and mosquito-borne illness before you go. The CDC’s traveler page for Ecuador is the public reference.
