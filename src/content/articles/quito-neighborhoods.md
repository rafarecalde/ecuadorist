Hotel links do not pay this site, so this page does not pretend to rank rooms. It compares neighborhoods. A booking site can show you a bed. The ride from the airport is [UIO Transfers](https://uiotransfers.com) or the [taxi and Uber comparison](/visit/quito-airport-arrival/). The days out are the [city and Mitad del Mundo tours](/book/quito-city-and-mitad-del-mundo-tours/).

<div class="compare cols-2">
<article>
<h3>Historic center</h3>
<p>UNESCO since 1978. Plaza Grande, San Francisco, La Compañía, the basilica. Hills. The UK FCDO says mugging and pickpocketing are common in the old town, so a pretty balcony is not a security plan. Stay here to walk to the churches. Eat early. The <a href="/visit/quito/">Quito chapter</a> is the loop.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>La Mariscal</h3>
<p>North of the old town, and where a lot of the newer restaurants and the nightlife sit. Convenient, and louder at night. Useful for a first stay if you want food within a few blocks and you do not need silence.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>La Floresta</h3>
<p>Residential, with the restaurant scene people mean when they say the city has a daily life. Calmer than La Mariscal, still central enough. A good default if the old town’s hills and the FCDO’s warning put you off sleeping there.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>La Carolina</h3>
<p>The big park, and the streets around it. North, modern, practical. This is the Quito of offices and a long green space, not of colonial stone. Day trips still leave from here. You will use a car or a taxi more than your feet.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>Guápulo</h3>
<p>The eastern edge, dropping into a gorge. It feels like a village glued to the city. Steep. Beautiful. A poor choice if you want flat walks after a flight. A good one if you want the view and you have already had your <a href="/visit/quito-altitude-sickness/">first quiet day</a>.</p>
</article>
<article>
<h3>Cumbayá</h3>
<p>The valley east of the city. Warmer, newer, and a commute. The <a href="/retire/best-cities/">cities chapter</a> says the same thing to people who might stay a year. For a week, choose it only if the hotel is the point and the old town is a planned outing.</p>
</article>
</div>

## What not to decide from a map

Neither the US advisory nor the UK page ranks these neighborhoods. The old town is the one a government page names. The rest of the comparison is how you will spend the day: walking, eating, or riding. The [food chapter for Quito](/eat/eating-in-quito/) names kitchens. [Where to stay](/eat/where-to-stay/) names a few real hotels, still without prices. A [ten-day route](/visit/ten-days-in-ecuador/) assumes you can leave the neighborhood for Cotopaxi without changing hotels every night.
