Guayaquil’s serious dinners split between Kennedy and Samborondón, the suburb across the river. These three are the rooms with that reputation. The [coast chapter](/visit/the-coast/) is the wider map, and the [safety guide](/visit/is-ecuador-safe/) is the one to read before a night in the city.
