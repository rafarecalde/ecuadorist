Guayaquil eats seafood in the north and sits down in hotel dining rooms downtown. These three are the rooms with a real following. The [coast chapter](/visit/the-coast/) is the wider map, and the [safety guide](/visit/is-ecuador-safe/) is the one to read before a night in the city.
