  - headline: "Light cars are off the road to Jujan"
    photo: nueve-octubre
    text: "From 15:00 on Friday, November 13, the Ministry of Infrastructure restricted light vehicles on the E-25 between Babahoyo and Jujan and named three alternates: Baba–Tres Marías–Salitre–Samborondón, Pueblo Nuevo–Simón Bolívar–Jujan, and Vinces–Macul–Palestina. By Saturday afternoon the water was still stopping light traffic, 44 centimeters deep at the Don Eduardo mill, about 25 centimeters at Ecuavegetal–La Carmela, and 10 centimeters at Mango Morado, while heavy vehicles were getting through and the prefecture had opened a side road of about seven kilometers, still under construction, for light cars."
    take: "Do not plan a rental-car drive south of Babahoyo on the E-25."
