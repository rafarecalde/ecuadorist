  - headline: "A Social Security form, before February"
    photo: quito-plaza
    text: "The U.S. Embassy said on October 22 that beneficiaries in Ecuador who were mailed a Foreign Enforcement Questionnaire on July 2 — people 90 or older, anyone with a representative payee, and Social Security numbers ending in 00 through 49 — can have benefits suspended beginning February 2027 if it is not returned, and that Ecuador’s mail may not have delivered it. The form can be completed by phone at +1-855-522-6936, weekdays from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern time."
