    photo: tortuga-bay
    text: "Heavy El Niño rains in the Galápagos have left one person dead and about 2,000 people affected, and authorities declared an emergency on Santa Cruz on Saturday, October 31. On Sunday the risk secretariat sent a second relief flight with 7.6 tonnes of aid, after 10 tonnes already delivered, 80 to 100 people were still in a temporary shelter as the rain eased, and a new storm warning runs until 10:00 on Wednesday, November 4, for Santa Cruz, San Cristóbal, Guayas, Los Ríos, and El Oro, with heavy rain also forecast for Pichincha."
